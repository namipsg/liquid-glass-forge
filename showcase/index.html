Liquid Glass Forge is an open-source material engine for the web. It refracts and diffuses an image, canvas, video or pixel source while your buttons, forms and links remain ordinary HTML.

What makes the glass optical?

Forge displaces and samples a known source through a shaped surface. The WebGL renderer and bounded CPU renderer provide refraction and color dispersion, alongside blur, tint and edge highlights.

Can it capture any HTML background?

The optical renderer needs an explicit source; Forge does not automatically rasterize arbitrary DOM content. This site uses known artwork and media for the optical examples, and Forge’s CSS material for ordinary page controls.

What happens when transparency is unsuitable?

Opaque material follows reduced-transparency and increased-contrast preferences. You can also select CPU optics or a CSS material fallback. The original controls keep their browser behavior.

What this measures

p95 shows how long the slower updates take. Completion age helps teams catch stale glass, while image storage and lifecycle checks help them plan for dashboards and repeated navigation.

Where qualification stands

SwiftShader and later A04s runs missed timing limits. Sustained GPU and mobile coverage remain open. These numbers apply to the test campaigns listed above.
