# Contributing

Liquid Glass Forge is currently in the feasibility phase. The [proposal](docs/PROPOSAL.md), [architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), [quality requirements](docs/QUALITY.md) and [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md) describe intended library behavior.

Propose focused changes with an observable outcome, relevant evidence and a verification approach. Label experiments, targets and supported behavior accurately. Changes to source access, optical equations, lifecycle or support policy should explain their effect on dependent packages.

Each behavior change should include a reproducible fixture or test, clear resource ownership and appropriate failure cases. Keep text and interactive content accessible through fallback and reduced-motion modes. Do not add a framework dependency to the neutral core.

The [Phase 1 spike](spikes/phase-1/README.md) has standalone unit, browser and benchmark commands. Production package, adapter and release checks remain future work. Documented requirements must not be reported as passing checks until their infrastructure and cases exist.

Keep local research and unpublished working material in ignored local directories. Stage specific public files and review the complete staged diff before publishing. Never force-add local private notes, credentials or raw captures. The repository's [license](LICENSE) applies to contributions; identify third-party code and preserve its required notices.
